# JA

A manga, manhwa and comic reader for Android, built on [Mihon](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon).

*Requires Android 8.0 or higher.*

## What JA adds

* **Auto scroll** in the long-strip (webtoon) reader, with a play/pause button and adjustable speed.
* **Google Drive sync** of the library, reading progress, settings, repositories and extensions between devices.
* **Extension sync**: extensions installed or removed on one device follow on the others.
* **Automatic migration** of entries whose source is no longer installed, with a summary and a revert option.
* **Duplicate clean-up** that keeps the most complete copy of each series.
* **In-app updates** from this repository's releases.

## Install

Download the APK from the latest release on this repository's **Releases** page and open it on your phone.
After that, JA tells you when a new version is out and installs it itself.

Google Drive sync needs a one-time setup in Google Cloud; see [SYNC_SETUP.md](./SYNC_SETUP.md).
